# Tài liệu tham khảo

## Đề bài và chuẩn kỹ thuật

1. HCMUS CS Teaching Assistants. [Yêu cầu chung](https://hcmus-cs-ta.github.io/lab-book/intro-ds/statement/), [Milestone 1 - Crawling](https://hcmus-cs-ta.github.io/lab-book/intro-ds/statement/crawling/), [Milestone 2 - Parsing](https://hcmus-cs-ta.github.io/lab-book/intro-ds/statement/parsing/).
2. arXiv. [API User's Manual](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/user-manual.html), [API Terms of Use](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/tou.html), [Full-text harvesting](https://info.arxiv.org/help/bulk_data.html).
3. Allen Institute for AI. [Semantic Scholar Academic Graph API](https://api.semanticscholar.org/api-docs/graph).
4. [QA Replies 2627-1](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11UDWf52DitCDrV386WkYK0ZySqTGQSCRnEwQ37u9uyQ/edit) của giảng viên môn Nhập môn Khoa học dữ liệu.
